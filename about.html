Who we are

Turning community ideas into a clear path forward.

Grant Bridge helps NGOs, community groups, and changemakers shape meaningful work into practical, fundable project proposals.

Our story

More than proposal writing. A bridge to action.

Good ideas are already making a difference in communities across Kenya. What is often missing is the structure and language to help those ideas reach the right partners and funders.

Grant Bridge closes that gap with practical support that is clear, collaborative, and grounded in the realities of local organizations. We help turn community priorities into organized plans that people can understand, support, and act on.
